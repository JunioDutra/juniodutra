This site has a clearer shape now.

The main page is now a focused portfolio, built to present who I am, the kind of engineering work I do, and a selected view of both company work and independent projects. The blog now lives under its own space, giving me room to publish notes, updates, and longer writing without mixing everything into a single page.

## What Changed

The biggest change in this refresh is structure.

- The portfolio lives at [me.dblsoft.xyz](https://me.dblsoft.xyz/)
- The blog lives at [me.dblsoft.xyz/blog](https://me.dblsoft.xyz/blog)

This gives the site two clear jobs:

1. The homepage introduces my professional profile quickly.
2. The blog gives me a place to write about projects, ideas, experiments, and technical work over time.

## Why I Wanted This

I wanted a place that felt more intentional.

Not just a list of links, and not just a repository index, but a small personal space that explains how I work, what I build, and where to find the things that matter most. A good portfolio should make the first impression easier. A good blog should make continuity easier.

This version is meant to do both.

## What You Can Expect Here

From here on, this blog can be used for a mix of:

- short updates about the site and personal projects
- notes on backend engineering, Java, Node.js, Python, and systems work
- experiments involving Linux, ARM devices, embedded hardware, or game development
- selected write-ups connected to things I build or learn over time
